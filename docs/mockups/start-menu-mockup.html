Start Menu

Clickable mockup of the panel applet. It's filled with the 66 apps installed on your laptop. The folders are your real App Library folders. The icons are placeholders, and the tile groups and "Most used" counts are examples. The Favourites are your real dock favourites. Changes stay in this browser.

Try

  • Type anywhere to search. Use ↑ ↓ and Enter to pick a result. Esc clears the search, and a second Esc closes the menu.
  • Right-click (or long-press on a phone) an app or a tile to pin it, resize it or move it.
  • Edit: tap a tile to pick it up, then tap where it should go. You can rename groups here too.
  • Tap a letter header to jump through the A–Z list.
